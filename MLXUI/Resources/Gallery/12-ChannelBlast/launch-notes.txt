AcmeSync 2.0 launch notes

- Calendar sync now works across Google, Outlook, and iCloud in
  one unified view.
- Conflict resolution is automatic; overlapping events prompt a
  single merge suggestion instead of duplicating entries.
- Built for small teams: shared team calendars are included at
  no extra cost, up to 10 seats.
- Pricing: $6/seat/month, billed monthly, no annual lock-in.
- Ships August 1st. Existing beta users are auto-upgraded.
