Employee Handbook (excerpt)

Working hours are flexible; core hours are 10am-4pm local time
for meeting overlap. New hires get 20 PTO days per year, prorated
in the first year. Expenses under $75 do not require
pre-approval. All laptops are company property and must be
returned within two weeks of departure. Security incidents must
be reported to security@company.example within one hour of
discovery.
